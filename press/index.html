Radio Bitcoin

A 24/7 Bitcoin radio station broadcasting in Portuguese — music, markets, Austrian economics and Bitcoin news, on air every hour of every day.

on air now

Why us

Latin America's Bitcoin conferences are covered almost entirely in Spanish and English. Brazil is the region's largest market — over 200 million Portuguese speakers — and has no Bitcoin radio covering these events.

We are not the biggest outlet in the room. We are the one that reaches an audience the others structurally cannot: Portuguese-speaking listeners, in audio, in their own language.

And unlike an article that is read once, radio coverage keeps replaying. A segment recorded at a conference stays in rotation for weeks.

What the station is

Radio Bitcoin runs 24 hours a day with 21 automated programs — market open and close for US equities, Austrian economics, commodities, survival & climate, Bitcoin news bulletins and an AI-hosted technology show. Original music sits between the blocks.

The programs are written and voiced by five rotating AI hosts, and listeners vote for their favorite. The entire station — streaming, scheduling, production and distribution — was built and is operated by one person.

That last part tends to interest people at Bitcoin conferences: it is a working demonstration of what an individual can run without a newsroom, a broadcaster or a platform in the middle.
